Meet Sarah Speller

✦

Sarah Speller is a trained archivist who has spent a decade working at Portsmouth Museum, building deep expertise in South Coast history and an exceptional talent for finding, interpreting, and communicating historical records.

Whether you want to understand who lived in your Victorian terrace, trace the wartime history of a commercial building, or compile a detailed family history, Sarah brings the same meticulous research and clear, engaging writing to every commission.

Secondary Service

Personalised Family History Packs

✦

In addition to building histories, we offer fully personalised family history research packs — custom-made to trace your family's unique story through archive records, census data, military records, parish registers, and more.

Every family history pack is tailored specifically to you, with detailed, accurate, and well-sourced research presented in a clear, readable format.
